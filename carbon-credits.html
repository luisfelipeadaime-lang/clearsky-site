The seal has three stages. Only one needs an outside party.

Stage 1 · Calculo.

Footprint calculated and registered with a reduction plan.

Stage 2 · Reduzco.

A downward trend in emissions intensity, over four consecutive registered years.

Stage 3, final · Compenso.

Compensation with registered Spanish absorption credits, from one tonne up.

Why buyers are moving now

Public tenders.

Contracting authorities can now award points for a registered footprint. Where they do, your registry entry is the proof (art. 10, RD 214/2025).

The annual cycle.

Large companies publish their footprint every year, with a five-year reduction plan, the same plan that qualifies you to compensate.

From September 2026.

EU rules end carbon neutral claims on products. Compensating at company level, through an official registry, is exactly what remains.

Three ways to buy

01 · Spot purchase.

Registered credits from our inventory, delivered immediately. We handle the transfer and manage your OECC compensation filing, end to end.

Best for: a first Compenso seal, or closing out this year's footprint.

02 · Forward offtake.

A multi-year supply programme. Lock volume and price for years to come, with deliveries scheduled to your compensation calendar.

Best for: recurring annual compensation, with price certainty.

03 · Project acquisition.

Acquire an entire project before registration. The first tranche of credits arrives at registration, 9 to 12 months after planting; the rest follows the registry's monitoring schedule.

Best for: the largest footprints, at the lowest cost per tonne.

Higher volumes and longer terms attract lower prices, and programmes are sized to your footprint, whether that is a few hundred tonnes or a whole project. Prices are quoted on request and are not published.

Annual programme · Plan Compenso.

Your compensation, renewed every year, without you touching the registry. Each year we size the purchase to your registered footprint, execute the transfer, file with the OECC and prepare your seal renewal. One invoice, no administration, and priority allocation on multi-year terms.
