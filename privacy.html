We process your personal data for the following purposes:

4. Legal Basis

We process your data on the basis of:

  • Consent: By submitting the contact form, you consent to our processing of your data for the purposes described above.

5. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for 24 months from the date of submission. After this period, your data is deleted unless there is an ongoing business relationship or legal obligation requiring further retention.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your data may be shared between ClearSky Limited and Clear Sky Carbono S.L. for business purposes related to your inquiry. We may use third-party service providers (such as form processing and email services) that process data on our behalf under appropriate data processing agreements.

7. Cookies

This website uses minimal analytics only. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party marketing cookies.

8. Your Rights Under GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@clearskyltd.com.

9. Supervisory Authorities

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with:

For any questions about this privacy policy or your personal data, contact us at info@clearskyltd.com.
