Land

We lease or buy your land, pay for the planting, and manage the forest.

The owner carries no cost. We fund planting, maintenance, monitoring and the registry work, and manage the forest for the life of the project.

How we contract

We contract directly with the owner or the forest association: no intermediary, no marketing fee. Mixed-species planting suited to the site, not a monoculture. Thirty-year minimum permanence, with monitoring at least every five years.

We work in Galicia, Asturias and Castilla y Leon, with a pipeline in Andalucia.

What happens when you contact us

1

Send us the location and rough size of the holding.

2

We diligence the site, at no cost to you.

3

You receive an offer in writing: lease or purchase.

4

We plant, register and manage. You are paid annually.
