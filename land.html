Land

We lease or buy rural land, fund the planting, and manage the forest.

The owner carries no cost and shares in the income. We fund planting, maintenance, monitoring and the registry work, and manage the forest for the life of the project.

How we contract

We contract directly with the owner or the forest association: no intermediary, no marketing fee. Mixed-species planting suited to the site, not a monoculture. Thirty-year minimum permanence, with monitoring at least every five years.

We work in Galicia, Asturias and Castilla y León, with a pipeline in Andalucía.

The process

1

The owner sends the location and rough size of the holding.

2

We diligence the site, at no cost to the owner.

3

An offer follows in writing: lease or purchase.

4

We plant, register and manage. The owner is paid annually.
