## Extraction Method
- Fetched `https://www.clearskyltd.com` via curl
- Extracted Wix static media URLs (`static.wixstatic.com/media/`)

- Downloaded at full resolution (removed Wix resize/blur transforms)
- Visually inspected each image to identify content
- Renamed with descriptive names
