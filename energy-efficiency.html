Energy efficiency · CAE

We pay for the upgrade. You keep a share of the savings.

Spain's 2026 energy-savings obligation is approximately 9,325 GWh, and up to 92% may be satisfied through CAEs. Obligated companies buy certificates rather than pay the penalty.

That demand funds the work. ClearSky covers up to 100% of approved capex on qualifying efficiency projects. The host pays nothing up front, keeps a share of every euro saved, and never touches the certificate process.

Source: 2026 BOE Order.

How it works

1

2

3

4

Certificates monetized, verified savings shared under contract

Qualifying measures include pumps and motors, lighting and controls, and heat recovery.
