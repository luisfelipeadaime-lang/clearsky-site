Energy Savings Certificates · CAE

We develop energy efficiency projects.

ClearSky develops the project end to end: assessment of qualifying measures, engineering, execution and certification. The host site shares in the verified savings under contract, without deploying its own capital.

Request an eligibility review

Under Spanish law, certificates are requested by obligated parties and accredited delegated parties. ClearSky structures and funds the works; issuance runs through accredited delegated parties, and our delegated-party arrangements are being completed ahead of first issuance.

The obligation behind the demand

Spain's annual energy-savings obligation runs to 2030 under the EU Energy Efficiency Directive. The 2026 national target is 9,325 GWh, of which up to 92% may be met with Energy Savings Certificates. The penalty for a shortfall is set at EUR 198 per MWh; certificates trade below it. That gap is why the demand is structural, not discretionary.

Certificates are issued against a catalogue of standardised measures, verified by an ENAC-accredited verifier, with the file presented to the relevant regional authority.

Sources: BOE, Orden TED/133/2026; RD 36/2023; EU Directive 2023/1791.

For the host site

We assess the installation and confirm which measures qualify. We coordinate engineering, execution and certification. The certificate is monetized, and the host site shares in the verified savings under contract.

The host site keeps a share of every euro saved and never touches the certificate process. Qualifying measures include pumps and motors, lighting and controls, and heat recovery.

For obligated parties

Certificates from projects we develop and execute ourselves, with volume and delivery scheduled against the buyer's annual obligation rather than sourced opportunistically.
