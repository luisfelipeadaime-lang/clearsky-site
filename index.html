How we work

Identify.

We look for holdings that carry cost instead of income: rural land with nothing on it, equipment that wastes energy. Each one is diligenced before anything else moves.

Develop.

We fund the work with our own capital and carry each project through Spain's state registry until it produces a registered, saleable asset.

Manage.

These are long projects. We manage them for their full life, and we remain the counterparty throughout.

Who we work with

Buyers.

Companies compensating a registered footprint buy credits directly from us. Obligated parties contract certificate volume against their annual obligation. Proposals within 48 hours.
