MITECO Reforestation

Credible Spanish Environmental Outcomes

Registered MITECO supply is fragmented across many project holders, while institutional buyers can struggle to obtain curated, project-backed, multi-year supply from one accountable counterparty.

ClearSky sources registered Spanish MITECO credits from established developers on a transaction basis.

At year-end 2025 the MITECO registry held 1,250 absorption projects, approximately 10.65M tonnes of forecast lifetime absorptions, and approximately 171,857 initial available tonnes used for compensation.

Source: Official MITECO 2025 Annual Report.

Registry participation follows a structured progression. Calculo (measure) establishes a baseline. Reduzco (reduce) demonstrates improvement. Compenso (compensate) allows offset of residual emissions. Participation signals maturity, not a purchase mandate.
