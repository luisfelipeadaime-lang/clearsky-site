What We Do

Three Business Lines

Sales & Trading

Own commercial strategy and revenue delivery across environmental credit markets. Spot, offtake, prepay, and options structures. Interface with demand, execute go-to-market, and drive pipeline to close.

Developments & Advisory

Originate, evaluate, and shepherd projects through pipeline to commercial readiness. Strategic advice, due diligence, and structuring to make projects shovel-ready for offtake or investment.

Investments

Principal and third-party capital management. Originate investable projects, structure vehicles, deploy capital for principal and investor returns. Celsius Carbon LP and co-investment vehicles.
