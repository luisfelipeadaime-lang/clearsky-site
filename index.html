ClearSky Limited is a global climate solutions firm backed by MKS PAMP, delivering durable, high-integrity carbon projects across nature-based solutions, permanent removals, plastics, and compliance markets.

We help corporates and investors meet the next generation of climate, compliance, and credibility challenges — capitalizing on inefficiencies in voluntary and compliance markets to deliver superior risk-adjusted returns.
